CULTURAL LANDSCAPE

Banaue Rice Terraces

The rice terraces of Ifugao demonstrate generations of traditional farming knowledge and water management. The wider Ifugao Rice Terraces include UNESCO-listed clusters.

📍 Banaue, Ifugao

MOUNTAIN WILDERNESS

Mount Pulag

One of the Philippines' best-known high mountains, famous for grasslands, forest ecosystems, and spectacular cloud-sea views. Climbers must follow park registration and guide requirements.

📍 Benguet and nearby areas

MOUNTAIN TOWN

Sagada

Sagada offers scenic mountain views, limestone caves, and distinctive local heritage. Visitors should use local guides for cave trips and follow community rules.

📍 Sagada, Mountain Province

NATURE

Apayao

Apayao features forested landscapes, river systems, and rich biodiversity. Explore only recommended areas and pay attention to weather advisories.

📍 Apayao Province

CITY & CULTURE

Baguio City

The regional center of CAR is known for its cool climate, pine trees, public parks, markets, and the annual Panagbenga flower festival.

📍 Baguio City, Benguet

HERITAGE & SCENERY

Abra and Kalinga

These provinces offer river valleys, mountain scenery, weaving traditions, and community-led cultural experiences. Ask local tourism offices about appropriate places to visit.

📍 Abra and Kalinga Provinces
