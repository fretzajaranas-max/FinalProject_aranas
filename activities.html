Activities & Experiences

Discover the Cordillera through outdoor adventures, local flavors, cultural experiences, and nature.

CHOOSE YOUR EXPERIENCE

Make Your Journey Meaningful

⛰️

Adventure

  • Hike designated mountain trails.
  • Enjoy scenic mountain viewpoints.
  • Explore caves with accredited guides.
  • Take nature walks in public parks.
🧵

Culture & Crafts

  • Learn about traditional weaving.
  • Discover local woodcarving.
  • Visit community-approved cultural sites.
  • Learn about local heritage from residents.
🥣

Food & Markets

  • Try fresh highland vegetables.
  • Taste heirloom rice and local dishes.
  • Explore Baguio's public market.
  • Buy locally produced food and crafts.
🌲

Nature & Photography

  • Photograph mountain landscapes.
  • Observe local plants and wildlife.
  • Enjoy pine-covered public spaces.
  • Watch sunrise or sunset from safe viewpoints.
🎉

Festivals

  • Experience Baguio's Panagbenga festival.
  • Learn about Mountain Province's Lang-ay Festival.
  • Discover Ifugao's Imbayah celebrations.
  • Check local announcements for current schedules.
🌾

Responsible Tourism

  • Support local guides and small businesses.
  • Ask before taking photographs of people.
  • Respect sacred places and traditions.
  • Keep trails clean and follow safety rules.

TRAVEL THOUGHTFULLY

Be a Guest, Not Just a Visitor

Every community has its own customs and traditions. Listen to local guidance, respect cultural boundaries, and help protect the region's natural beauty.
