SAMPLE TRAVEL PLAN

A Three-Day Baguio Itinerary

This sample focuses on Baguio City. Destinations such as Banaue and Sagada require separate travel planning because of the distance involved.

Day Morning Afternoon Evening
Day 1
Arrival		 Arrive in Baguio and check in. Visit Burnham Park and the public market. Try local food and rest.
Day 2
Explore		 Visit a public garden or pine-lined park. Explore local crafts, shops, and markets. Enjoy dinner and prepare for the next day.
Day 3
Departure		 Enjoy breakfast and revisit a favorite spot. Buy local products and prepare to travel home. Departure, depending on your transport schedule.

BE PREPARED

Travel Tips

  • Check weather forecasts before travelling.
  • Allow extra time for winding mountain roads.
  • Use registered transport and local guides when required.
  • Bring cash for small shops and local markets.
  • Respect Indigenous customs and sacred places.
  • Keep the environment clean and follow trail rules.

PACK SMART

Packing Checklist

  • Warm jacket or sweater
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Raincoat or umbrella
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Personal medicines
  • Phone charger and power bank
  • Valid identification and cash
  • Sun protection
  • Reusable shopping bag
  • Small first-aid kit
