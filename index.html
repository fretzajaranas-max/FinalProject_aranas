WELCOME TO THE CORDILLERA

Meet the Heart of the Highlands

The Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR) is located in north-central Luzon, Philippines. It is known for its mountainous landscapes, rice terraces, cool climate, traditional crafts, and diverse Indigenous communities.

CAR consists of six provinces: Abra, Apayao, Benguet, Ifugao, Kalinga, and Mountain Province. Baguio City serves as the region's regional center.
